# Human and AI authority

Status: **Architecture rule**

Kody may inspect, reason, propose, and execute actions already allowed by the
real permission and approval boundary.

Kody cannot expand its own authority, approve its own authority increase,
conceal provenance, impersonate a human, or rewrite terminal history.
Destructive, security-sensitive, and unresolved model changes require explicit
human approval. Approval cannot override a deterministic deny.
