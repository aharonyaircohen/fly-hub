# Policy resolution

Status: **Current guidance plus older Engine enforcement**

Dashboard Policies and Constraints are Markdown guidance. They can affect a
prompt, but prose alone is not deterministic enforcement.

The published Engine's older Loop dispatch resolves a structured policy,
approval, budget, and concurrency snapshot. The simplified Dashboard Agency
contract does not yet expose that same structured policy model.

Future consolidation must keep deterministic denies, approvals, budgets, and
capacity at dispatch boundaries. It must not turn Policy into another public
Agency model unless reuse and enforcement require it.
